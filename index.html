Hello, I’m Cole Cerull, a recent graduate from the University of Colorado Boulder with a BA in Computer Science and a minor in Geography. My passion lies in combining technical expertise with sales to provide customized solutions for clients.

I founded an automotive business in high school, where I specialized in reprogramming engine control units (ECUs) and installing aftermarket parts. Working with clients to understand their needs, I delivered tailored solutions that enhanced vehicle performance. This hands-on experience taught me the importance of communication in sales and how to drive results by aligning technical expertise with customer goals.

I also worked for Boost Dynamic Tuning, where I further honed my technical sales skills, managing the entire sales cycle from product recommendations to customer support. I enjoy bridging the gap between technical solutions and customer satisfaction, ensuring that every client leaves with a solution that meets their performance needs.

In addition to automotive technology, I’m passionate about fabrication, including welding, machining, and 3D printing, which I use to build custom parts for my projects. Outside of work, I enjoy spending time in the mountains, skiing, and camping, always seeking new challenges and ways to innovate.

I’m eager to bring my skills in technical sales and problem-solving to a dynamic team. Feel free to explore my projects or reach out to discuss how I can contribute to your company’s success.
